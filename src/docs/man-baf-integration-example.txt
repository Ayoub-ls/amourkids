THINGS THAT WON'T BE HIGHLIGHTED WORD-FOR-WORD, ON PURPOSE:
  - "1500" and "5000" are written as one token each but you'll SAY several
    words for them ("ألف و خمسمية", "خمسة آلاف"). Expect the highlight to
    drift by a word or two across that one sentence, then resync at the
    next punctuation. This is a known limitation, not a bug — if it bothers
    you once you see it, tell me and I'll show you how to keep that one
    sentence highlighted as a whole unit instead of word-by-word.
